HOW IT WORKS

  1. 1

    Hunt

    Type the Pokémon and pick the game, which sets the odds. Then pick your emulator's window.

  2. 2

    Keys

    The keys your emulator uses. mGBA, DeSmuME, melonDS, Azahar and more fill in for you.

  3. 3

    Steps

    The reset loop it plays. Start from a GBA, DS or 3DS soft reset and tweak the waits.

  4. 4

    Area

    Drag a box around the spot where the Pokémon shows up in battle.

  5. 5

    Check

    Pause on a normal one and press ADD FRAME. Every encounter gets compared to it.

Then press START or F8 from anywhere. It only presses keys while your emulator is the active window, so clicking away pauses it.

FEATURES

Any emulator

AUTO

It types like a keyboard and looks at the screen like a screenshot. No plugins, no memory reading, no patched ROMs.

Shiny check

AUTO

Compares each encounter to the normal colours, with a live meter and a SUGGEST button that sets a safe threshold from your real scores.

Alerts anywhere

ALERT

A Discord message with the screenshot, a push to your phone through ntfy, and a heads-up if a hunt stops on its own.

Real numbers

STATS

Resets, time, pace per hour, your chance of having found it by now and how long until 50% and 90%.

Made for streams

STREAM

Text files for OBS with the count, odds, pace and phase, plus the Pokémon's sprite. Mini mode keeps a small counter on screen.

Safe and yours

SAFE

Saves as it goes with a daily backup, never touches your game files, and keeps every hunt on your PC.

SCREENS

The keys work like the ones on the device. Pick a page to see it.
